## Decisions made (and why)
- Goroutine-per-puzzle with disjoint-index writes + `WaitGroup` (no append-to-shared-slice) → race-free by construction (jasnah mutation-proved: a shared-append variant fails `-race`).
- `MaxBatchPuzzles = 256` doubles as the goroutine-count bound (no unbounded fan-out); count-cap is the primary 413 path (before solving); `MaxBytesReader` is the byte-cap defense (`errors.As` on `*http.MaxBytesError`).
- `SolveParallel` == `Solve` via the SAME engine loop (see the dedup below) with the parallel compute strategy injected — determinism/byte-identity is load-bearing and structurally guaranteed.
- No durable/architectural decision surfaced → no ADR promoted.
